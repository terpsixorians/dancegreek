About the group

Founded in 1994, the TerpsiXorians Hellenic Dance Group of New Jersey is a volunteer-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to preserving Greek dance and customs. Featuring dancers ages 13 and older, the group performs at cultural and charitable events, with donations funding community programs and authentic regional costumes.
